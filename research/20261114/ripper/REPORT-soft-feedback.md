# H1bc: aligned soft feedback passes recovery and finite alignment targets

The two prespecified endpoints are **separately supported**. Aligned soft feedback recovers **31/32 certified integral exact graphs**, exceeding the procedure target of 29. Cell-permuted feedback recovers **26/32**, so the exact-bank difference is **+5**, exceeding the separate alignment target of +4. Both bounds are resolved, with no fractional or unresolved new outcomes. The procedure and alignment statements should not be combined into a broader causal claim.

Aligned feedback wins on six paired banks and loses on one. The remaining aligned failure, bank 10, has two missing and two extra edges. The permuted arm has fourteen missing and fourteen extra edge-bank pairs across its six failures. Same-arm top-810 controls recover 0/32 exact graphs in both arms, with 217 missing/extra pairs for aligned costs and 245 for permuted costs. Per-cell degree structure remains consequential.

This is an adaptive mechanism follow-up on the same examined H1az source rows and mask-independent corrupted boards, after uniform and exposure-balanced acquisition failed their frozen targets. No source identity or latent draw is new. Both recipes, the exponent, all seeds and per-bank cell permutations, endpoints, proof rules, and budgets were frozen before generating outcomes. There is no post-hoc rule or exponent tuning, and this is not fresh-source confirmation.

The greedy mask policy uses

\[
w_{ij}=\frac{(8/9)^{m_{ij}}}{1+c_{ij}},
\]

where m is the pair's past coquery count and c is its past observed equality count. The control replaces only the feedback matrix with `c[permutation, permutation]` for a fixed independently seeded permutation of 81 cells. Each arm maintains its **own actual observed history**. Its joint counts remain in true cell coordinates, and the final recovery always uses its true observed equality/coquery counts. Both arms share the prior H1ba/H1bb tie permutations and the same underlying corrupted board per row.

There is no calibration, hard exclusion, likelihood-ratio state, or supplied noise-probability parameter. The alphabet-dependent 8/9 exposure rule and exponent one are fixed design choices. Before each current source or latent is accessed, the policy hashes its actual past state and finalizes the mask. Independent replay checks that ordering, every state digest, mask, noise draw, selected label, and count update. For each of 64 bank-arm streams, a future-suffix gate changes sources and latents from row 450 onward and reruns acquisition; the first 450 masks, labels, and count state remain identical.

Recovery is unchanged: all 3,240 pairs eligible, exact half-even rounded Laplace equality-rate costs, and degree exactly 20 at every cell. Degree and the implied 810-edge cardinality remain supplied structural priors; geometry is used only for evaluation. The experiment demonstrates one specific calibration-free acquisition/recovery procedure clearing its finite target on these streams. It does not remove the structural prior or guarantee success under arbitrary noise.

The alignment intervention changes where feedback acts and the resulting history, including exposure and feedback-magnitude pathways. Its +5 endpoint supports a finite procedure-level advantage for this aligned rule over this frozen permutation control. It does not identify a unique mediator, establish population significance, or show that the location of label information is the sole mechanism. In particular, a procedure success alone would not have established the alignment endpoint.

All **64 new and 64 archived exact primal-dual certificates** pass. Archived adaptive and balanced LPs are not refitted. No primal is snapped, and certificates establish optimality rather than uniqueness. A fractional returned optimum would have counted as a procedure failure without ruling out an integral co-optimum. The independent public verifier rejects 384 altered certificates. It checks exported mask exposures, permutation seeds, costs, proofs, outcomes, and separate endpoint bounds; sequential policy/label replay and future-suffix isolation require the raw archive.
