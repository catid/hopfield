The statistic is label-free: for exact doubled LP objectives `F(0),...,F(40)`, let `κ(d)=F(d+1)+F(d−1)−2F(d)` for degrees 1 through 39, and `R=max κ /Σκ`. An all-zero denominator is defined as `R=0/1`; it did not occur here. Comparisons use exact integer cross-products and independently agree with rational `Fraction` comparisons. Strict ties fail. Each bank succeeds only when **both** mask arms have `R_real>R_null`. A false arm comparison makes the joint bank a resolved failure even if its other arm were unresolved; otherwise unresolved comparisons retain conservative endpoint bounds. Here the bounds are [32,32].

All **32 examined H1bq real banks and both mask arms** are retained, irrespective of their real graph accuracy. This is a conditional archived-real/matched-null study, not fresh real-source confirmation. For each bank, a fresh 180×81 board array has independent discrete-uniform symbols 0 through 8, generated by the frozen NumPy 2.3.4 stream. Both archived masks observe the same latent null board. No extra corruption is needed: symmetric independent digit corruption preserves the uniform single-cell law. Uniform masks query 72 cells independently each row. Balanced masks are the H1bq randomized two-cycle `GF(9)²` affine-line complements, with exactly 142 coqueries per pair.
