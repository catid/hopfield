# H1af: bounded evidence tilt while preserving exposure pressure

H1ae's LR-only selector failed both frozen accuracy endpoints. Its post hoc diagnostic found 3,386 never-queried nonedge-bank pairs and median exposure 2 among retained nonedges in the learned arm, compared with zero never-queried pairs and median exposure 258 for its exposure control. This motivates a new policy; H1ae's negative result remains unchanged and is not pooled into this study.

The four arms are exposure/exact, bounded/exact, exposure/learned, and bounded/learned. Every arm uses actual independent replacement noise eta=.05, N2220, 25 training queries per board, q1=1/9, LR crossing boundary 648000 and edge error budget .005. Each learned arm shares its bank's one M1000 repeated-query calibration, paying 57,500 queries in total; exact controls pay 55,500. The selector comparison has equal query costs within each calibration type.

For an unexcluded pair, let m and c denote past joint-query and noisy-equality counts. From its frozen calibrated bound, compute logLR=m*logB+c*(logA-logB), where A=q1/q0 and B=(1-q1)/(1-q0). The candidate weight is (8/9)^m times exp(clip(logLR/2,0,log16)). Excluded pairs and the diagonal have zero weight. FP64 order is fixed: compute the stated logLR expression, divide by 2, clip to [0,log16], exponentiate, and multiply by numpy.power(8/9,m). The exposure control uses its unchanged (8/9)^m weight.

The evidence factor lies in [1,16], corresponding to at most log16/log(9/8), approximately 23.54 joint exposures of priority. This is a bounded priority advantage, not a theorem that every pair is queried or that the final greedy allocation is balanced. Factor16 is fixed for this study without a parameter sweep. The same greedy25-cell rule selects the largest weighted degree first, then maximizes weight to the already selected cells. All ties follow the shared frozen permutation.

The policy receives only past joint/equality counts, irreversible exclusions, k25, a tie permutation and fixed calibration log multipliers. It receives no current label, source identity, geometry or oracle handle. History arrays are read-only and hashed before and after each mask call. Every arm's current mask finalizes before current noise is generated or revealed. Independent replay reconstructs masks, ties, counts, weights and all first-crossing paths from archived queried observations. Finite weights, the multiplicative bounds, extreme counts and all-tied/all-excluded cases are preflighted. The floating weights do not decide exclusions: exact rational crossing thresholds remain unchanged.

For a true edge, clean labels never agree and the noisy equality probability is q0(eta)=eta/4-9eta²/64. On valid calibration, eta<=eta_bar<=8/9 makes the supplied null bound conservative. The fixed LR multiplier has conditional expectation at most 1 under the true-edge null when q0_bound<q1. An unqueried pair has multiplier1. Since inclusion is determined by past observations, the e-process remains a nonnegative supermartingale. Ville's inequality and the union bound over 3,240 pairs at boundary648000 give deletion risk at most .005. Calibration failure at most .005 gives risk at most .01 per learned model. Shared calibration does not create a single joint1% guarantee across selectors or banks. The safe eta_bar=8/9 fallback retains every pair. All calibration outcomes, including undercoverage and fallback, remain in the analysis.

The primary endpoints over32fresh banks are: bounded/learned minus exposure/learned accuracy at least10percentagepoints; bounded/learned absolute accuracy at least70%; and zero wrong certified stops in both learned arms. Gain is supported if its marginal paired two-way95%lower bound is at least10pp, falsified if its upper bound is below10pp, and otherwise inconclusive. Usefulness is supported if its marginal95%lower bound is at least70%, falsified if its upper bound is below70%, and otherwise inconclusive. Any wrong certified stop fails the stop gate. The conjunction is supported only if both interval endpoints and the gate pass, falsified if either interval endpoint is falsified or the gate fails, and otherwise inconclusive. Both intervals use5,000bootstrap draws over banks and the same10,000puzzleindices, with no simultaneous-coverage claim. Exact controls and other contrasts are descriptive.
