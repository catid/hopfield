# H1ae: likelihood-ratio evidence for acquisition at fixed query cost

H1ac found that M1000/N2220 learned calibration at 5% independent replacement noise was not useful. H1ad restored usefulness by doubling training to N4440, at 113,000 learned-model queries. This study returns to M1000/N2220 and tests a fixed acquisition rule that uses past noisy equality evidence. Previous studies remain separate and unchanged.

The four arms form a selector × calibration factorial: exposure/exact, LR/exact, exposure/learned, and LR/learned. Every arm uses actual independent replacement noise eta=.05, N2220, 25 training queries per board, q1=1/9, the same LR crossing boundary648000 and edge error budget .005. Each learned arm reuses the bank’s one M1000 repeated-query calibration, paying 57,500 queries in total; exact controls pay 55,500. No training or calibration budget is increased to help the candidate.

For an unexcluded pair, let m and c be its past joint-query and noisy-equality counts. Given the fixed calibrated noise bound, logLR=m logB+c(logA-logB), with A=q1/q0 and B=(1-q1)/(1-q0). The candidate uses weight exp(min(logLR,log648000)/2). Excluded pairs and the diagonal have zero weight. The first selected cell has largest weighted degree; each subsequent cell maximizes its total weight to the already selected cells. Every tie follows the shared frozen permutation. This is exactly the old greedy25-cell rule with new weights. The exposure control continues to use (8/9)^m on unexcluded pairs.

The policy interface accepts only past joint/equality counts, past exclusions, k25, the tie permutation and the frozen calibration log multipliers. It has no current-row label, source identity, geometry or oracle handle. The collector marks history arrays read-only and fixes all four masks before generating or revealing any current training noise. Thus each pair’s inclusion indicator is predictable from the past, even though the policy uses its own past e-process evidence. Independent replay reconstructs every mask from archived queried labels and verifies the event order.

Clipping occurs before exponentiation, so weights are finite and at most sqrt648000 up to floating-point rounding. Very negative logLR can underflow to zero without overflow or NaN. The FP64 evaluation order is frozen as m*logB+c*(logA-logB), followed by the upper cap and division by2. Read-only tests, all-tied/all-excluded cases, extreme valid counts and independent greedy tie reconstruction gate acquisition. Actual log-LR crossing decisions still use the unchanged exact rational integer thresholds, not the floating selector weights.

For a true edge, the clean labels never agree. Its noisy equality probability is q0(eta)=eta/4-9eta²/64. On valid calibration, eta<=eta_bar<=8/9 makes the supplied null bound conservative. For q0_bound<q1, the expected LR multiplier under the null is at most1. An unqueried pair gets multiplier1. Since every mask is fixed from past labels before the current noise, the product remains a nonnegative supermartingale for either selector. Ville’s inequality and the union bound over3,240pairs at boundary648000 give edge-deletion probability at most .005. Calibration failure at most .005 gives total deletion risk at most .01 per learned model. Shared calibration across selectors does not create a single joint1% event across models or banks. At eta_bar=8/9, the unchanged safe fallback retains every pair. All calibration outcomes, including undercoverage, are kept.

The primary endpoints over32fresh banks are: LR/learned minus exposure/learned accuracy at least10percentagepoints; LR/learned absolute accuracy at least70%; and zero wrong certified stops in both learned arms. The gain endpoint is supported if its marginal paired two-way95%lower bound is at least10pp, falsified if its upper bound is below10pp, and otherwise inconclusive. The usefulness endpoint is supported if its marginal95%lower bound is at least70%, falsified if its upper bound is below70%, and otherwise inconclusive. Any wrong certified stop fails the stop gate. The conjunction is supported only when both interval endpoints and the stop gate pass, falsified if either interval endpoint is falsified or the stop gate fails, and otherwise inconclusive. Each interval uses5,000bootstrap draws over banks and the same10,000puzzleindices; no simultaneous-coverage claim is made. Exact controls and other contrasts are descriptive.

All streams, weights, endpoints and sample counts freeze before the implementation-only old-board pilot or fresh fits. Calibration and training source pools are globally disjoint within this study; all previous Ripper source rows are excluded. Independent source, endpoint, threshold, mask/tie, noise, first-crossing and support audits gate inference. New replays cache noise arrays once, mark them read-only and verify unchanged bytes, using the independently validated optimization with pinned provenance. Earlier audits and scientific runners are preserved.

The edge guarantee does not ensure the gain or usefulness endpoints, exact graph recovery or valid capped returns, and is restricted to the stated independent replacement-noise family.
