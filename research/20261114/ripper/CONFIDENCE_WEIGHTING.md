# H1ah: confidence weights for returned-board accuracy

H1ag's fixed/learned models had 10.20% canonical zero-energy compatibility, 9.43% correct certified returns, and another 10.31% correct capped returns whose targets had positive energy. Compatibility limits the relevant certificate outcome, not returned-best-board accuracy. This study tests whether uncertain retained constraints should receive weaker positive penalties. Earlier studies remain unchanged and are not pooled.

Each of 32 fresh banks uses one learned-bound exposure fit at actual independent replacement noise eta=.05, M1000 calibration pairs, N2220 training boards and 25 training queries per board. Its cost is 57,500 queries. The same fit, sources, supports, inference inputs and search seed are shared by three inference arms. Training is not repeated three times, and the three uses of a fit are not independent training samples.

For each retained pair, compute its final fixed-q1=1/9 likelihood ratio using the learned null bound. The uniform arm assigns weight 1 to every retained pair. The evidence arm assigns weight 1 when the **exact rational LR is at most 1**, and weight 1/16 otherwise. Equality at LR=1 receives weight 1. Excluded pairs have weight 0 in every arm. The classification compares integer numerator and denominator products directly; floating logarithms do not decide weights. The safe no-pruning calibration fallback has LR identically 1 and therefore uniform strong weights.

The shuffled control uses exactly the same multiset of weights as the evidence arm. A dedicated random stream generates a full permutation of all 3,240 pair indices before any calibration or training observations. After fitting, restrict that order to retained pairs and assign the first K retained indices weight 1/16, where K is the number of weak evidence weights. All other retained pairs receive weight 1. Only K is read from the evidence assignment; its weak-pair identities do not enter placement. This per-model permutation is fixed for every puzzle and trajectory, with a stream separate from training noise, acquisition ties and search randomness. No geometry or held-out label enters weight assignment.

Acquisition retains the unchanged exposure weights (8/9)^m, greedy 25-cell rule, shared tie permutation and exact fixed-q1 crossing boundary 648000. Masks see only past joint counts and exclusions and are fixed before current noise generation or reveal. Calibration and edge testing each have error budget .005. Under the declared noise family and valid calibration, the existing predictable-mask supermartingale proof gives at most .01 true-edge deletion risk per shared learned fit. All calibration outcomes, including undercoverage and fallback, are retained without filtering or refitting.

All retained weights stay strictly positive, so reweighting preserves the zero-energy set and every given–given conflict. If the support retains all true Sudoku edges, a clue-preserving zero-energy board is a valid Sudoku completion. The weights are exact binary fractions with numerators in {0,1,16} over 16. Minimum positive energy 1/16 exceeds stopping tolerance .001, so a nonnegative energy below the threshold must be zero. Independent scaled-integer field, energy, focus and certificate audits check this arithmetic. Canonical-solution compatibility is reported without an independent uniqueness claim; it is not an upper bound on positive-energy capped-return accuracy.

The strong penalty unit remains 1. There is **no per-model or mean-weight normalization**. All arms use temperature .4 annealed toward .31 with horizon 524288, focus theta .45, ebar=0, tau=0, tolerance .001, four chains and 262144 moves per chain. The shuffled control separates information in weight placement from the changed weight multiset. Hidden-target checks ensure target labels do not calibrate energy, temperature, focus or stopping. General and sparse search must agree for nonuniform weights, including an incremental state before periodic refresh. Exact integer fields and stopping are checked on old/synthetic preflight inputs and each fresh model before its main inference.

Primary endpoints are: evidence minus uniform returned-board accuracy at least 10 percentage points; evidence minus shuffled accuracy at least 10 percentage points; evidence accuracy at least 70%; and zero wrong certified stops across all three arms. Each gain is supported if its marginal paired two-way 95% lower bound reaches 10 pp, falsified if its upper bound is below 10 pp, and otherwise inconclusive. The absolute endpoint is supported if its marginal 95% lower bound reaches 70%, falsified if its upper bound is below 70%, and otherwise inconclusive. The conjunction is supported only when all three interval endpoints and the stop gate pass; falsified when any interval endpoint is falsified or the gate fails; otherwise inconclusive. Each interval uses 5,000 bootstrap draws over 32 banks and the same 10,000 puzzle indices, with marginal nominal 95% coverage and no simultaneous-coverage claim.

Primary accuracy is exact match to the stored canonical solution and includes correct capped returns. A wrong certified stop means an invalid Sudoku completion or a clue violation; a valid noncanonical completion, if observed, is reported separately and is not an invalid certificate. The report separately gives certified accuracy, correct capped accuracy, canonical compatibility, given–given infeasibility and compatible-target misses. These decompositions are descriptive. The frozen cohort is full-all [380000,390000). Exactly 96 model/seed/input cells are requested; only complete weight bytes, seed, inputs and all settings being identical permits reuse. The total budget is 30 GPU-min, including standalone GPU preflights; their measured allocation is subtracted from the four-GPU inference envelope. There is no extension or tuning after outcomes.

All rules, streams, endpoints and sample counts freeze before study source draws, pilot/fresh fits, study weight assignment or access to the reserved test targets. Fresh source roles are globally disjoint within this study and exclude all 1,628,683 prior Ripper source rows. Independent source, calibration, threshold, mask, noise, first-crossing, exact weight and permutation audits gate inference. Six old-board implementation pilots are separate and not pooled. BP AdamW is outside this inference-weighting mechanism study and is not run.
