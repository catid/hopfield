# H3bz proposal: match the inference prior to uniform truth sampling

Prospective proposal only. No protocol is frozen and no truth draw, source draw or actual oracle call is authorized by this document. The cohort size and endpoint were chosen before H3by's final outcome. H3by keeps hierarchical inference weights under uniform truth sampling; H3bz tests whether changing the inference weights helps, with the deeper policy otherwise fixed.

The single joint finite-cohort hypothesis is that on all 1,024 fresh paired cases, the uniform-fitted deeper arm U uses at least 2% fewer total actual membership calls than the hierarchical-fitted deeper arm H, while losing no exact identification that H achieved. Support requires both `100*U <= 98*H` and, for every case, `H_exact implies U_exact`. A complete valid run violating either condition falsifies the joint hypothesis; both component outcomes and all four exact-flag combinations are reported. H=0 gives an inconclusive cost endpoint. A resource cap, invalid certificate, missing case or implementation correctness failure leaves the whole study incomplete. No sample extension, threshold change, outcome-dependent early stop or exclusion is permitted.

Cross-arm exact equality is **not** assumed as a correctness invariant: different fitted weights can change MAP choices, handoff and terminal identification. U-only exact identifications are allowed by the prespecified no-regression endpoint; any H-only exact identification violates it. Neither total exact count equality nor average accuracy can substitute for per-case no regression. Every failure remains in the cost denominator, so lower calls achieved by losing even one H exact identification cannot support the joint claim.
