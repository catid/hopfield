# H3by proposal: robustness to a shifted truth prior

Status: prospective proposal. No new truth/source draw or membership-oracle call has occurred. H3bx's frozen deeper policy passed its fresh matched-prior 2% endpoint, using 9,975 calls versus 10,238 for its independently executed original baseline. This study keeps both fitted policies fixed and changes only the truth-sampling law.

The sole scientific hypothesis is that the fixed deeper policy still uses at least 2% fewer total actual membership calls than the fixed original four-MAP/depth-four posterior policy on all 4,096 new paired cases. Let D and B be the sums of every paid MAP and tree query in the two arms. The exact endpoint is `100*D <= 98*B`, using a newly executed baseline. If B is zero, the endpoint is inconclusive. A complete valid run missing the inequality falsifies this finite-cohort threshold. A cap, missing proof, or correctness failure leaves the entire study incomplete. No stopping based on the observed effect, sample extension, threshold change, exclusion or subgroup endpoint is allowed.

## Sampling prior and fitted prior are different

Truths are uniform over all 1,522 source-eligible models with M >= 8, retaining the full 1,541-model catalog and the 19 ineligible rows. Each eligible candidate receives exactly one truth-sampling ticket, in candidate-ID order. The expected orbit-count proportions are 1/1,522 for k=0, 55/1,522 for k=1, and 1,466/1,522 for k=2. This substantially increases the two-orbit share compared with H3bx's hierarchical sampling prior.

The fitted inference prior remains byte-for-byte the original hierarchical choice: k uniform over {0,1,2}, then uniform among C(55,k) models, conditioned on M >= 8. Both selectors and tree objectives retain the exact reciprocal score `1 / (C(55,k) C(M,8))`. They do not receive the new uniform truth prior, true orbit count, realized cohort frequencies or actual truth identity. Under the actual uniform sampling law, the correctly matched posterior would instead be proportional to `1 / C(M,8)`; that posterior is deliberately not substituted in either fixed arm.

The source law is unchanged: conditional on the sampled truth, draw eight distinct positive N12 boards uniformly without replacement. The complete N12 populations, supplied 1,541-model class and all 73,712 N13 query boards remain unchanged. Each pair shares its single source bank, giving 32,768 new positive appearances and 65,536 arm appearances.
