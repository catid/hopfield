# H3bx draft: fresh matched-prior confirmation of deeper handoff

Status: prospective design for review. No fresh truth/source draw or target oracle call has occurred. H3bw is complete and audited on the previously examined H3br cohort; this study proposes a new cohort after fixing the policy.

The sole scientific hypothesis is that the fixed H3bw policy uses at least 2% fewer total actual membership calls than a separately executed original H3br four-MAP/depth-four posterior policy on all 4,096 new matched-prior cases. Write D and B for the sums of all MAP and tree calls in the deeper and baseline arms. The prespecified decision is `100*D <= 98*B`. If B is zero, the result is inconclusive. A complete valid run that misses the inequality falsifies this finite-cohort hypothesis. Any resource cap, missing proof, or correctness failure leaves the entire experiment incomplete and produces no efficacy decision. No sequential stopping, sample extension, subset endpoint, or exclusion is allowed.

## Population and draws

Use the same supplied class of 1,541 models, the same complete N12 positive populations, and the same 73,712-board N13 membership universe. The declared hierarchical prior is uniform over k in {0,1,2}, then uniform over the C(55,k) choices of leaper orbits, conditioned on source cardinality M >= 8. It has 1,522 eligible models and exact integer weights 1485, 27 and 1 for k = 0, 1 and 2, respectively; total mass is 4,436 tickets. All 1,541 rows, including the 19 ineligible rows, remain in the population and frequency reports.

For case i in 0..4095, use `default_rng(2026900000+i).integers(4436)` and exact cumulative half-open ticket intervals in candidate-ID order. Independently use `default_rng(2027000000+i).choice(M, 8, replace=False)` to select eight distinct positive boards for that truth. The proposed 8,192 seeds have no collision with the 17,058 explicitly recorded integer seed values in 82 existing research protocols; the gate will preserve protocol identities and its precise search boundary. It does not claim to certify undocumented RNG streams.

Draw and durably log all 4,096 truth/source pairs before any prospective membership query. Each pair uses one shared source bank in both arms: 32,768 newly drawn positive appearances and 65,536 arm appearances. Retain repeated truths and unordered source-bank duplicates, including any match to historical eight-board banks. Do not redraw or suppress duplicates. Report all counts, source hashes, source indices, seed/ticket records, zero-frequency eligible models and ineligible models.

## Fixed policies and execution boundary

Execute the deeper arm for every case first, then execute the original baseline arm for every case. The baseline starts each case from the eight positives and an empty queried-board list. It never inherits the deeper arm's candidates, answers or MAP prefix. Its oracle is called again even when both arms select the same board. Query-overlap annotations only describe earlier committed same-case query metadata; their answers are never used for free routing. Each actual query is committed and fsynced before the separate oracle read, followed immediately by its hash-linked answer.

The deeper arm is byte-identical H3bw policy computation: after m paid MAP calls, stop at singleton; sets of at most 16 candidates may compile the exact posterior-optimal tree of requested depth 8-m. Larger sets continue the fixed MAP policy. For a feasible tree, evaluate every hypothetical original-baseline continuation, with 4-m remaining MAP calls and a terminal depth-four tree. Retain all candidate costs and exact flags, including zero-call terminal failures. Handoff requires every baseline candidate branch to identify and the exact integer expected-cost comparison to pass; equality accepts. At m=4 a feasible tree must tie. No genuine baseline branch or experiment case may be removed to make handoff admissible.

The separately executed baseline performs up to four unchanged MAP calls, stopping at singleton or absence of an informative board. If unresolved, it uses the original exact posterior-optimal depth-four tree, with the original lexicographic tie rule. A set of more than 16 candidates has a binary-information infeasibility certificate; other infeasible sets retain their full original feasibility proof and make no tree calls. The baseline compiler uses the original H3br full-enumeration source with a separate within-arm cache, rather than reusing a counterfactual result as its realized execution. Prefreeze fixed synthetic checks will compare its feasibility, costs and every candidate path with the new arm's depth-four counterfactual trees.

Both policies keep the reciprocal score `1 / (C(55,k) C(M,8))` over every compatible supplied model. No true identity, true orbit count, future label, cohort frequency or other arm's realized result enters selection or compilation. The supplied full prediction matrix remains declared model information; the actual truth identity is restricted to the separate membership oracle/evaluator. The deeper arm may retain its existing within-run canonical tree reuse and counterfactual caches; the baseline does not share those caches.

## Correctness, resource accounting and evidence

Every paired exact-identification flag must agree. This is a conditional correctness invariant of the admissibility rule, not the scientific endpoint. Under the prescribed matched posterior, the complete admissible baseline belongs to the deeper tree class, giving an expected-cost nonincrease property. Neither property establishes a realized 2% saving. No fixed success or failure count is assumed for the new cohort.

Retain the original H3bw all-inline compiler, normalized depths, exact lower-bound ordering and lexicographic pruning. Every Huffman bound is recomputed, every actual merge is charged, every full merge trace is retained, and the persistent bound registry must remain empty. Serialize complete proofs before compact routing caches. Preserve distinct requested-depth inputs, canonical source-proof identities, charged work and logical proof counts. The baseline's independent compiler states and split evaluations are added to the same global resource counters.

Proposed aggregate limits remain 600 CPU seconds, 900 wall seconds, 2,000,000 tree/counterfactual/bound memo states and 50,000,000 partition/merge/counterfactual-MAP work steps across both arms, gates, preparation, monitoring and serialization. Each arm has at most eight paid queries per case and 32,768 total; the two-arm maximum is 65,536. Runtime synthetic reference and original-baseline work are included. This is an independently budgeted new experiment, not an extension of H3bu/H3bv or a continuation of H3bw. No completion promise is made. Publication compression and independent post-run auditing are separately timed. GPUs have no eligible work for these CPU exact-search operations.

Before freezing, repeat the complete prior-ticket/cardinality/seed checks without calling the prospective RNGs; validate original-baseline/depth-four parity, the all-inline proofs, canonical reuse, cost guards, infeasible-branch retention, resource caps, ledger ordering/tamper rejection and integer serialization on fixed synthetic/archived fixtures. Repeat scientific gates inside the monitored run before the new draws. The independent completed-run verifier must replay both realized arms, source tickets/sampling, every proof and exact flag, query-ledger order, source and compiler identities, all counters and the integer primary. All 4,096 case statuses and every completed artifact survive an interrupted run.

Publish a compact package with all cases, both paid-query ledgers in the single append-only chain, figures, source identities and independent audit receipt. Keep large full proof payloads outside Git with exact raw/compressed identities and explicit portable-verification limits.

## Interpretation

H3bw's 222-call saving is only prior design evidence; its 10,355-call comparator and 10,147-call threshold do not enter this new primary. The new threshold is computed solely from the newly executed B. Report paired costs, totals, exact/failure counts, MAP/tree composition, handoff depths, duplicates, overlap, guards, workload counters and runtime descriptively. BP AdamW is not measured and remains the first row of method comparison tables. A passing result supports the frozen 2% threshold on this new finite cohort under the declared matched prior. It does not establish a uniform population effect size, arbitrary-rule learning, positive-only learning, or learned-optimizer superiority.
