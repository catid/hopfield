# Independent local-factor verification of H2ay

H2ay's original full audit reached its frozen 300-second budget. Its 48 inference jobs and raw archives remain unchanged. H2az is a separately frozen verification and performance experiment, with no new scientific input or inference trajectory.

For a deletion set C, the original energy is a sum of unary factors, pair-table factors, and directed attention-link factors. Factors with neither endpoint in C are identical before and after deletion, so they cancel exactly in the energy difference. For each directed offset d, affected factor sources are the union C ∪ (C−d), clipped to valid source/destination coordinates. Taking the unique source coordinates counts a factor once when both endpoints change. The verifier evaluates its original before/after table entries or directed products directly in long double; it does not reuse the production flip/quadratic scorer. Self-links use d=0 and occur once. Unary changes apply only to free deleted cells, so endpoint/wall clamps remain intact. The binary entropy term is unchanged by a 1→0 deletion and cancels.

This is an algebraic rearrangement of the same real-valued objective, with independently checked floating-point error. The full-grid implementation remains immutable as a reference. Full final energies, complete native fixed-point margins, graph checks, candidate geometry, published cost ordering and policy choices are still checked by the independent audit. Only the expensive full-grid calculation of each candidate's deletion difference changes.

Before freezing, the gate checks all 3^9 selected/deleted configurations on a 3×3 grid, including empty changes; separate wall/endpoint and rectangular boundary fixtures; all 168 long-chain H2aw candidates; and the first 344 H2ay candidates, including the prespecified first 256. The 512 actual deletions form a fixed benchmark fixture set. They are selected by source archive order, without new outcome selection.

The primary performance endpoint is a median paired process-time speedup of at least 2× across three alternating-order repetitions on the same fixed 512 deletions. Setup/loading is outside the timed region for both implementations. The full first H2ay job is also audited by both old and new verifiers, each with a separate 120-second ceiling; only two complete equivalent audits permit a measured end-to-end speed claim. Timeouts remain data. The complete new H2ay audit has its own 600-second, sixteen-process limit and saves each completed row atomically as it finishes. Neither timing endpoint changes H2ay's scientific thresholds or retroactively passes its original 300-second verification gate.
